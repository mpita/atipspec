---
name: atipspec-spec
description: Guide an ATIPSpec requirement discussion and write observable scenarios for a reviewable proposal.
---

Run `atipspec spec <slug>` and follow its workflow. Read existing context before
asking questions, at most three per round. For guided deliveries keep behavior in
specs/, and the intent, what is out of scope and the quality attribute references
in the delivery. Rewrite what `atipspec check <slug>` flags. Finish the approach
and test strategy before presenting one combined proposal. Never invent human
approval or silently choose unresolved business rules.
